Every day, small shops and farmers throw away produce that could have been sold, donated or reused.

For Prompt Wars x Creative Community VVITU I built Dukaan Saathi, an AI Freshness Clock.

Take one photo of a stall. Gemini Vision reads the produce, starts a countdown of how many hours it has left, suggests a discount, and points to the nearest place it can still be used: a tiffin centre, hostel mess, community kitchen, dairy or compost point. Farmers can speak what they have in Telugu or English and get matched to a nearby buyer.

Built with Google AI Studio and the Gemini API, deployed on Google Cloud Run.
